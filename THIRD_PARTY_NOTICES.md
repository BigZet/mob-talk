# Third-party components

JLayer 1.0.1 (JavaZoom), GNU Lesser General Public License 2.1, is included unchanged to decode public MP3 voice previews locally. The license text is included separately as `licenses/LGPL-2.1.txt`; the original dependency JAR contains no license file. Corresponding sources: https://repo.maven.apache.org/maven2/javazoom/jlayer/1.0.1/jlayer-1.0.1-sources.jar. Project: http://www.javazoom.net/javalayer/javalayer.html.

Voice names, IDs and descriptive metadata originate from the public ElevenLabs Voice Library. Public preview access and OpenRouter synthesis availability are tracked separately. The mod does not bundle generated voice audio or voice recordings.
