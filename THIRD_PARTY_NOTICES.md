# Third-party components

JLayer 1.0.1 (JavaZoom), GNU Lesser General Public License 2.1, is included unchanged to decode public MP3 voice previews locally. The license text is included separately as `licenses/LGPL-2.1.txt`; the original dependency JAR contains no license file. Corresponding sources: https://repo.maven.apache.org/maven2/javazoom/jlayer/1.0.1/jlayer-1.0.1-sources.jar. Project: http://www.javazoom.net/javalayer/javalayer.html.

Voice names, IDs and descriptive metadata originate from the public ElevenLabs Voice Library. Public preview access and OpenRouter synthesis availability are tracked separately. The mod does not bundle generated voice audio or voice recordings.

MCA Reborn (Minecraft Comes Alive Reborn, https://github.com/Luke100000/minecraft-comes-alive), Copyright (C) WildBamaBoy, Luke100000 (Conczin) and the MCA Reborn contributors, GNU General Public License 3.0: the human-villager renderer (model, skin, face, clothing and hair layers, genetics) is ported and modified, and its skin, face, hair, clothing and colour-map textures are included unchanged under `assets/mobtalk/human`. Details and the list of derived files: `third_party/mca-reborn/NOTICE.md` in the source tree.

uPlot 1.6.31 (https://github.com/leeoniya/uPlot), Copyright (c) 2022 Leon Sorokin, MIT License: the chart library of the web admin page, included unchanged as `assets/mobtalk/web/uplot.min.js` and `uplot.min.css`. The license text is included as `licenses/uPlot-MIT.txt`.

RoadWeaver (https://github.com/shiroha-233/RoadWeaver), Copyright (c) 2025 shiroha-233, MIT License: a reference for the village roads; the street lamp's shape follows its `LamppostDecoration`. No source file is included. Details: `third_party/roadweaver/NOTICE.md`; the license text is `licenses/RoadWeaver-MIT.txt`.

NiftyCarts (https://github.com/jmb05/nifty-carts), Copyright (c) 2019 MennoMax, (c) 2023 jmb19905, MIT License: the cart and wagon models of the village roads, ported to yarn in `third_party/nifty-carts/src/client/java`. Details: `third_party/nifty-carts/NOTICE.md`; the license text is `licenses/NiftyCarts-MIT.txt`.

skniro's Furniture (https://modrinth.com/mod/skniros-furniture), Copyright (c) skniro, MIT License (as declared in the mod's fabric.mod.json and on Modrinth): the shape of the village bar's chair (`assets/mobtalk/models/block/bar_chair.json`, generated by `tools/bar/bar.py`) follows its `normal_chair` model; no texture is copied. The license text is included as `licenses/skniro-furniture-MIT.txt`.

Divine Block (a mod by the author of Mob Talk, source in the author's repository divine-block), GNU General Public License 3.0: the effects sprite atlas `textures/effect/fx.png` (glow, spark, star, ray, ember) is included unchanged as `assets/mobtalk/textures/gui/cards_fx.png` for the card table's light and sparkle effects. The card faces, backs, chips, table, frame and table sounds of the game of 21 are drawn and synthesised by the project's own scripts `tools/cards_art.py` and `tools/cards_sounds.py`.
